ABOUT

About Me

I am an electrical and electronic engineering researcher interested in photonics, nanophotonics, optoelectronic devices, and energy technologies.

My research interests include dielectric metasurfaces, silicon photonics, subwavelength structures, electromagnetic simulation, optical filtering, and photovoltaic devices.

I am particularly interested in designing and optimizing nanoscale photonic structures for improved optical and optoelectronic performance.
